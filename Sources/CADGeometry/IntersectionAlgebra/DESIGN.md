# Intersection Algebra

## Purpose and Scope

Child of [CADGeometry](../DESIGN.md), without children. Owns exact original
rational intersection equations and ideal preparation. General real component
decomposition and public SSI/CS closure remain required; finite real fibers and algebra preparation
alone completes neither intersection requirement.

## Responsibilities and Boundaries

This task composes one real-relation authority for all seven intersection/contact
requirements. The rational native chart receipt is its first composing unit,
not an all-family or public result completion claim. Required source laws remain
those enumerated below; typed refusal cannot complete mandatory support.
Parameter connectedness, original Jacobian rank and geometric image dimension
remain separate facts. Whole relation selection preserves exact boundary
inclusion and excludes denominator-zero cells without changing source equations.
Projected consumers retain original screen equality and real fibers; self-contact
consumers retain full source-product equality before subtracting only proved
structural identities. Consumers own their geometry semantics, never a second
copy of the decomposition engine.

Owns the polynomial ring, ideal preparation and native homogeneous embedding.
Original native producers retain their validated source/domain authority.
No fitted, rounded or interval coefficient becomes an original source value.
Numerical tracing, public intersection publication and upper features are outside
this component.

## Architecture

```text
original native laws + original selected domains
  -> homogeneous embedding
  -> three coincidence equations + denominator guards
  -> exact lexicographic ideal
  -> real decomposition / parameter lifting / whole-domain coverage
```

## Contracts and Invariants

### Admitted Original Coordinate Rings

The original polynomial ring has a positive caller-selected coordinate count.
The first nine exponents remain inline. Higher coordinates use an immutable,
canonical overflow suffix: trailing zero exponents are omitted, equality and
hashing are independent of unused ring slots, and the original ring count owns
coordinate validity. Every overflow allocation, traversal and copy is admitted
before mutation. Exponent, ring-count and quantified-ring arithmetic is checked
for integer overflow. Work/storage admission, not a guessed fixed coordinate
capacity, bounds a computation. Existing public rational geometry relations
continue to expose one through four geometric coordinates; this does not bound
the internal rings required by exact coefficient transport or closure proofs.

All polynomial operations preserve the complete exponent sequence, including
coordinate injection, differentiation, division, ideal normalization and
projection. Lowering may discard only proved-zero suffix slots. The closure
formula uses two copies before certified reductions of the admitted source ring and one radius;
its dimension is checked before building the quantified proof. Whole-cell
closure and finite-branch/affine sufficient theorems have no separate inline capacity.
No unsupported dimension is silently truncated or classified as absence.

Recursive GCD support uses an unsigned inline word and an admitted sorted
overflow coordinate list. A coordinate beyond the machine word cannot disappear
through a shifted bit mask. Pseudo-division constructs its degree shift using the
complete original coordinate power, including non-inline main coordinates.
Remapping keeps inline targets on stack scalars and reuses one admitted scratch
buffer per polynomial for larger targets; each retained overflowing monomial
owns its canonical immutable suffix.

Equality first compares immutable canonical term counts, then compares their
ordered entries until the first difference. Each inspected entry and overflow
power is admitted; neither unequal term counts nor an early difference requires
a full traversal of both tables. Derivatives issue one coefficient table directly:
subtracting the coordinate unit is injective among surviving source monomials,
so intermediate monomial polynomials and repeatedly sorted prefix sums are
unnecessary. The full output table and canonical ordering remain caller-admitted.

Each admitted polynomial publication retains one canonical descending term table
alongside its immutable coefficient lookup. The table's allocation and sorting
are paid once by that publication; leading-term reads and subsequent ordered
traversals share it without allocating another sorted table. Lifted/lowered
polynomials retain the same immutable tables after their coordinate proof.
No mutable lazy cache, shared lock bypass or independent allocation quota is
introduced. Projection may reuse an existing square-free normalized witness
when the incoming monic law matches it exactly before recomputing a GCD.
Within one projection level, each reductum retains its issued original axis
coefficient table. Principal subresultant pairs share these tables rather than
extracting both operands again for every pair. The table issuer owns the source
and axis; mismatched axes/rings are rejected. The projection-level owner pays
each table once and releases it with that level's preparation state.

This change owns only the exact coordinate representation and the consumers
that previously assumed its inline size. Ordered coefficient producers and
geometric public composition remain separate unfinished work. Verification
crosses coordinate nine in original arithmetic, lexicographic division,
injective remapping, full real projection and incidence, checks overflow and
independent work/storage rejection, and reuses the existing rational suites to
reject changes to their original laws and results.

### Original Ordered Coefficient Authority

`OriginalIntersectionCoefficient` owns transport of one actual retained ordered
field value to a rational auxiliary law. Its issuer computes the exact norm of
X minus that original value, preserves original denominator saturation, and
checks that the resulting rational polynomial annihilates the original value.
It enumerates all distinct real roots under coefficient admission and uses exact
original-value comparisons with their retained rational isolations to select
exactly one root. A numerical enclosure or approximate coefficient cannot issue
this receipt. The original field value remains retained with its annihilator
and selected root; no conjugate is inferred from a rounded sample.

The geometric and coefficient root-depth domains remain independent. The
original value's retained context must fit both unchanged caller limits.
Original-field sign operations retain their own root admission domains; norm
root enumeration and subsequent coefficient-root refinement spend coefficient
admission only. Reuse never resets the original context's inherited coefficient
depth. Rational constants bypass auxiliary roots. All arithmetic and retained
storage use the same caller ledger; failure publishes no coefficient receipt.

`OriginalOrderedIntersectionPolynomial` retains each original ordered coefficient
with a validated full geometric monomial. Differentiation changes that monomial
and scales its actual retained field coefficient by the exact integer degree;
rank/critical witnesses remain original laws supplied explicitly by their owner.
A nullified fiber remains whole unless such a witness requires its subdivision.
The relation compiler assigns auxiliary
coordinates only to nonrational coefficients, before the geometric suffix, and
retains a mapping from every original term to its coefficient issuer. Each
auxiliary coordinate is constrained by that issuer's annihilator and exact
selected isolation, including excluded endpoint facts. Even separate auxiliary
coordinates for correlated values remain fixed to their actual original values;
conjugate products are never admitted as alternative input coefficients.

`OriginalRationalIntersectionCells` retains a coefficient-prefix count, independent
coefficient-depth limit, and both achieved depth domains per cell. Only prefix
root enumeration/separation spends coefficient admission. Later geometry roots
spend parameter admission and inherit the source's parameter depth. Lifting may
not relabel a root after issuance or lose the original source coefficient depth.
The selected coefficient prefix must consist entirely of zero-dimensional
sections. The compiler checks its source-bound prefix and projects the immutable
published sample to the geometric suffix; whole cell conditions still retain
the original auxiliary authority. A zero coefficient count uses the identical
rational execution path, without an invented coefficient-cell visit.

The ordered relation composer places fixed coefficient coordinates before
geometric coordinates, binds each to its issued original polynomial and selected
real isolation, and retains the source laws and parameter suffix. Those fixed
coordinates have dimension zero in the selected relation and never become
geometric parameters. Closure candidates quantify geometric coordinates only;
the coefficient prefix is shared original authority, not a second independent
coefficient embedding. The fixed-fiber composition is owned below; the public ordered publication
contract is owned by Public Ordered Relation Binding. Whole-family source and
portable completion remain separate obligations.

Verification must distinguish positive/negative conjugates, algebraically equal
and different coefficients, coefficient-only towers and retained parameter-root
sources, repeated/reducible defining laws, saturated denominator poles and
independent work/storage/parameter/coefficient depth failures. Actual input
producers, rather than rounded values reconstructed in fixtures, must reach the
issuer.

### Closure Over Fixed Original Coefficients

The derivative-closed cell condition retains the issued coefficient-prefix cell
index. An ordered source cell requires a zero-dimensional coefficient prefix;
a free coefficient sector cannot be treated as fixed original authority.
Affineness for a fixed fiber counts degree only in geometric coordinates.
Coefficient-only laws keep their original strict signs, and candidate cells
must have exactly the same issued prefix. Convex interpolation in the geometric
suffix then proves weak affine signs sufficient for closure, while fixed
coefficient laws remain unchanged. A polynomial affine in the full ring is the
zero-prefix instance of the same theorem.

For c fixed coefficients and g geometric coordinates, the general closure
formula uses c+2*g+1 coordinates: the original coefficient prefix, a free
geometric tuple, one positive radius and one candidate geometric tuple. The
candidate remapping shares each coefficient coordinate with the free point;
only geometric differences enter the neighborhood box. Because the source
coefficient prefix is one closed point, this is exactly the original ambient
closure rather than a relaxed coefficient-dependent solution set.
Every original candidate sign remains an explicit guard. Source zero conditions
still form the free closed-support equations, and original free partition laws
make incidence membership constant on each whole refined cell.

A source-issued linear equation `A*q+B=0` in the last candidate coordinate,
together with a retained strict original sign for A, proves exactly one candidate
q = -B/A for every remaining candidate tuple satisfying that guard. The generic
quantified path substitutes this identity into every predicate by exact
denominator clearing with the positive multiplier sign(A)*A. Every zero
condition other than the selected equation remains a substituted zero condition;
it is not reduced by its own ideal and discarded. The selected equation becomes
the identically true zero atom. All box constraints, source signs, radius and
free closed support remain in the program. Only this proved unique existential
coordinate is removed; the original candidate map and A/B receipt retain its
reconstruction. A zero or unproved leading sign never issues this reduction.
This exact quantifier elimination is part of the general neighborhood formula,
not an affine or finite-branch sufficient theorem or post-exhaustion fallback.

A quantified free-prefix lift may reuse the issued suffix of its full projection
tower. Before reuse, every requested equation/partition witness must match a
retained prefix source after exact monic square-free normalization; a missing
witness refuses reuse before lifting. The first prefix source array preserves
its original equation and partition offsets, and all lower projection laws are
lowered only after proving their discarded suffix powers zero. The same root
lifting, sign evaluation, original support, caller ledger and inherited depths
remain authoritative. A foreign source order or ring fails explicitly. This is
reuse of an unchanged full projection proof, not a second CAD or quota.

Quantified free-prefix lifting retains the coefficient count, unchanged
coefficient limit and both achieved source depths. Radius/candidate roots spend
parameter admission only, while original coefficient signs retain their own
issued domain. Fixed coefficients are never duplicated into independently
selected conjugates or relabeled as geometric roots. Callers may disable affine
and finite-branch theorems before execution to verify the actual general formula;
exhaustion does not select an alternative theorem afterward.

The actual generic ordered path is verified through closure incidence for a
half-line endpoint and a two-dimensional fixed-coefficient region, with complete
refined-cell truth, conjugate exclusion, nonfixed-prefix rejection and independent
failure behavior. Public ordered publication is owned by Public Ordered Relation Binding.

### Public Ordered Relation Binding

The public input owns one explicitly discriminated rational or ordered law list.
`OriginalOrderedPolynomial` retains full original geometric monomials whose
coefficients are exact immutable `OriginalAlgebraicCoordinate` values. A public
cell sample may be reused as one exact scalar, never as a whole-cell range.
Exact rational scalars have a rational-field issuer; original source producers
retain their actual field value rather than reconstructing it from an enclosure.
The original sample's achieved parameter and coefficient depths travel with the
value, including when exact simplification yields a rational constant or zero.
Sample enclosure/refinement accepts both explicit limits and retains both
achieved domains; parameter causality is owned by the existing persistent value
isolation, while coefficient refinements map from the original context to the
retained coefficient baseline. No second parameter mapping is applied.
Polynomial differentiation and zero-term removal retain those causal baselines.

Coefficient transport maps each original-field parameter visit to the retained
source baseline plus refinement beyond the original context depth. Its internal
parameter limit is the remaining caller allowance plus the context depth.
Coefficient visits use their independent unchanged caller limit; inherited
coefficient depth is retained even when it exceeds the field's stored depth.
Reuse may share a coefficient axis only when both retained depth authorities
are covered by the reused issuer; algebraic equality does not justify a shallower causal receipt.

The public resolver's non-generic protocol requirement accepts both explicit
depth limits. Rational input uses the same zero-coefficient decomposition;
ordered input uses the complete source-bound compiler, closure and incidence.
Publication checks the exact retained ordered compiler/partition binding before
issuing the common result. Public samples omit auxiliary coordinates, public
signs refer to input laws only, and fixed coefficients add no image dimension.

Exact rational geometric membership obtains the issued fixed coefficient-prefix
point for each candidate cell. It evaluates the complete cell condition in that
prefix's original field with rational geometry appended, never substitutes a
rounded coefficient and never inherits unrelated geometric sample roots.
Independent parameter/coefficient limits apply to both retained baselines and
new sign refinements. Foreign identifiers, cancellation and budget exhaustion
fail before publication or membership success.

This port's behavioral evidence must execute the public existential requirement,
source sample reuse, exact opposite-conjugate exclusion, geometric-only samples,
ordered region/isolated-point components, rational geometric membership, source
and caller depth rejection, work/storage failure and foreign-owner failure.
This port does not establish every existing analytic/procedural source producer
or arbitrary quantified-budget completion. Those remain separate fixed gaps.

### Public Original Rational Relation Selection

`OriginalPolynomialRelationResolving` is the public witness-table boundary for
complete rational relations in the existing one-through-four original-coordinate
ring. `OriginalRationalPolynomial` owns immutable original rational laws;
`OriginalPolynomialPredicate` owns a validated postfix sign program bound by
index to that retained law list. `OriginalPolynomialRelation` binds both to
exact closed or unbounded rational coordinate domains. Strict/open boundaries,
unions, denominator exclusions and structural diagonals are explicit sign
predicates, never rounded cuts. This contract does not cover nonrational source
producers or establish the completion of any entire intersection requirement.

The concrete resolver partitions the complete original law list and domain
boundaries with derivative-closed cell identity before evaluating the sign
program. Truth is constant on each full cell. Selection retains the complete
ambient partition, the original program and every selected full cell. Closure
and component assembly use those issued selected cells; excluded boundary
contacts cannot connect components. Selection cannot be reapplied silently to
a different law list or replace a previous selection receipt.

`OriginalRealRelation` retains the input, complete selected partition, incidence
and connected components. Public cell identifiers refer only to this immutable
receipt. Representative coordinates are explicitly cell samples, never bounds
on a whole cell or a substitute for its sign condition. Exact algebraic sample
enclosures retain the original field value and inherited depth. Rational point
membership is checked against original cell conditions; callers can inspect
zero-dimensional or positive-dimensional components without converting complex
ideal classification or original Jacobian rank into image dimension.

`OriginalRelationUsage` forwards all work, UInt32-word retention, geometric-cell
and coefficient-cell admission to the composing callbacks. Its copies retain
the same callback owners; no private quota or mutable shared state is introduced.
Non-generic protocol requirements remain usable through Native/WASM/Embedded
existentials. Invalid rings/programs, foreign cell identifiers, cancellation,
overflow and independent limits fail before atomic result publication.

Verification owns the public protocol path, real isolated points in positive
complex-dimensional ideals, vertical/crossing/cusp components, open boundary
disconnection, boolean unions, denominator exclusions, exact nonrepresentable
endpoints, algebraic sample refinement and independent caller failures.

Linear original-root comparisons use the existing exact Sturm variation law at
the expression's unique zero. A uniquely isolated root lies to its left exactly
when the original interval-to-station count is one, and to its right when zero;
an admitted in-interval zero of the original polynomial proves equality. Closed
and excluded endpoints retain their original ownership. This replaces repeated
localization solely to decide a linear sign, not the caller depth limit or the
admission of coefficient signs. Persistent scalar refinement then spends causal
depth on actual value bisections rather than restarting the same root search.

CS coordinates are (t,u,v); SSI coordinates are (u1,v1,u2,v2). One additional
coordinate can express denominator saturation. Exponents are nonnegative and
checked for overflow. Higher indexed coordinates have lexicographic priority.
Zero terms are absent; reduced bases are monic and deterministic.

For every spatial coordinate the equation is H1*W2-H2*W1=0. Both original
denominators remain retained. Saturation adds z*W1*W2-1=0, excluding components
where either denominator vanishes. Source/domain owners survive cancellation
of every coincidence equation.

A unit ideal proves algebraic absence. A pure leading power of every coordinate
proves zero complex dimension. Missing pure powers indicate positive complex
dimension. Neither is complete real-root lifting or geometric image dimension.

The foundation must support every existing Curve3D/Surface3D source family,
isolated/regular/critical/coincident strata, whole-domain coverage, exact closed
incidence and original periodic/pole semantics. A successful graph join requires
shared original solution identity and branch incidence. Representative proximity,
overlapping intervals or endpoint curvature times a gap do not establish identity.
Numerical residual and whole-component proof are distinct obligations. Empty
results require exclusion of the whole requested domain. Exhaustion or unresolved
required coverage cannot return partial success. Upper feature repairs are excluded.

### Intersection Classification and Proximity Boundary

This is the approved target contract; it is not a claim that the existing
public implementations already satisfy it. The workspace policy is linked
from the parent module design. Algebra preparation continues to own equations
and ideals only; composing intersection/proximity producers own the obligations
below.

| Contract | Assumption | Guarantee | Failure |
|---|---|---|---|
| Original intersection | Authoritative source laws and selected domains are retained | All original common points/curves and their parameter fibers are covered; tangent and singular contacts remain distinct from absence | Unresolved coverage cannot publish complete success |
| Original coincidence | A common image region and both source domains are identified | Whole-region source equality and parameter correspondence are established, including differing parameterizations | Small residuals, parallelism within angular tolerance, and samples alone cannot establish coincidence |
| Proximity | The metric, threshold, and compared domains are explicit | Certified distance bounds and corresponding source locations/regions justify the threshold decision | A bound straddling the threshold is unresolved, not silently accepted |
| Empty intersection | The whole selected product domain has been examined or otherwise excluded | No original common point exists in the requested domain | A failed search or exhausted allowance is not an empty result |

Intersection classification describes the common geometric image and retains
parameter-fiber dimension separately. In particular, a collapsed parameter
region is not automatically an image coincidence region. Proximity is a separate
relation that may hold alongside exact intersection; query kind and guarantee
remain explicit. Equality at a proximity threshold is accepted only when the
specified non-strict bound is established. No numerical residual tolerance is
an equality predicate.

```text
retained original sources and selected domains
  -> original intersection/coincidence proof -> original relation and coverage
  -> independent proximity proof            -> metric bounds and correspondence
                    -> explicit modeling repair policy
                    -> changed geometry/connectivity and deviation evidence
```

The original coefficients/laws determine identity. Numerical enclosures control
representation error; modeling tolerances bound a proximity query; input-source
uncertainty is separately retained when provided. None changes the authored
source law implicitly. Filtered numerical predicates may accelerate a decision
only when their bounds prove it; uncertain cases refine under the same caller
allowance or fail explicitly. Arbitrary-precision work does not imply an
unbounded time or storage entitlement.

The existing plane-pair tolerance branch and original interval-exclusion branch
currently have different public semantics. Migrate them through explicit result
contracts and dispatch, with caller migration recorded rather than a silent
change to the meaning of the existing coincidence result. This document resolves
the design decision, not those unimplemented migrations. General legacy source
coverage remains required before foundation completion.

Verification must distinguish separated parallel planes within the proximity
threshold, truly coincident planes, a shallow-angle real intersection, exact
tangency and nearby disjoint geometry. The same authoritative sources must
produce the same original classification through every public dispatch route.
Additional checks must reject transitive merging of pairwise-near entities,
unproven whole-region equality, threshold-straddling distance bounds, and partial
coverage reported as complete. Exhaustion and cancellation must remain explicit.
These are future behavioral completion obligations, not tests already passed.

### Finite Real Fiber Contract

`OriginalFiniteIntersectionFiber` consumes a verified zero-dimensional ideal
or a unit ideal. It owns complete enumeration of distinct real parameter tuples
within the requested coordinate domains; it does not classify contact order or
positive-dimensional real components. A positive-dimensional complex ideal is
rejected explicitly rather than called an empty real fiber.

For coordinate k, lexicographic elimination supplies all basis equations involving
only coordinates 0...k. Substitution of retained preceding exact roots gives
univariate polynomials over that root's ordered field. Their common real roots
are enumerated using exact GCD and the existing complete Sturm root issuer.
Pure leading powers for every coordinate ensure a nonzero monic equation at
each stage; loss of this invariant is an explicit failure. A constant GCD excludes
that whole prefix. The root issuer's global coefficient bound covers all real
roots; no arbitrary search window is introduced.

Each extension retains its defining root and embeds earlier coordinates into
the same descendant field. Closed domain boundaries are compared by exact signs
against their original Double coefficient values. Final tuples must satisfy every
retained original generator exactly. Repeated algebraic roots are emitted once;
the original ideal remains retained for the later contact-order owner.

A linear fiber uses its exact parent-field quotient rather than adding a
redundant algebraic extension. Every coordinate retains its original root witness
separately, including linear coordinates. The source ideal, root witnesses and
coordinate tuple therefore survive this exact representation reduction.

```text
verified original ideal
 -> all real coordinate-0 roots
 -> specialized coordinate-1 common roots over each retained field
 -> remaining coordinates and exact closed-domain exclusion
 -> check every original equation -> complete finite parameter tuples
```

One caller ledger owns all stages, root visits, field allocations and final
tuples. Its parameter-root depth is passed unchanged; coefficient-root depth is
not invented for original rational coefficients. Failure in any stage discards
the whole publication. A cancellation-checking adapter holds the exclusively
borrowed ledger value during lifting and restores it on both return and throw;
it forwards all four admission operations without creating or resetting an
allowance. Tests must cover irrational towers, distinct roots with
the same preceding coordinate, repeated roots, complex-only fibers, exact closed
endpoint inclusion, excluded domains, cancellation and root/work/storage limits.

### Full Rational Projection Preparation

`ExactIntersectionProjection` owns a full Collins projection polynomial set;
its result is preparation for sign-invariant lifting, never a real-component
or intersection publication. Unlike restricted well-oriented projection, it
retains degree changes and nullification witnesses. The construction follows
[the full projection operator and principal subresultant matrix definition](https://ceur-ws.org/Vol-1974/RP2.pdf).

`ExactIntersectionProjectionTower` applies this operator from the highest
original coordinate down through coordinate zero and retains every level's
source/output set. Original coordinate slots are preserved rather than renamed.
The last level has no nonconstant projection output; its univariate sources
remain available for complete root lifting. No level publishes representative
samples as a whole-cell proof.

For each member of the exact square-free computational support basis, retain
every nonzero reductum obtained by removing
its current highest-axis coefficient. Project each reductum's leading coefficient
and all principal subresultant coefficients with its derivative. For each pair
of input polynomials, project principal subresultants of every pair of their
reducta. Axis-independent input polynomials pass to the lower level unchanged.
Only identically zero or nonzero rational constant supports are omitted. Nonzero
outputs retain their exact square-free supports, normalized by a rational scalar;
no parameter-dependent content or distinct factor is lost from the arrangement.
Original input laws and multiplicities remain in the retained source list.
Duplicate suppression checks exact polynomial equality.

For degrees m and n, principal index j uses the first n-j shifted rows of the
first polynomial and the first m-j shifted rows of the second, restricted to
the first m+n-2j columns of the Sylvester matrix. Its exact determinant is
computed by fraction-free Bareiss elimination over the remaining polynomial
ring. Row permutations retain their determinant sign. Every division is exact
polynomial division with a strictly descending leading monomial; a nonzero
remainder is an explicit invariant failure. The empty determinant is one.

```text
original rational polynomials
 -> all nonzero reducta and leading coefficients
 -> derivative and pair principal subresultants
 -> exact lower-coordinate projection set
 -> sign-invariant lifting (separate completion obligation)
```

The same caller pays coefficient extraction, reducta, matrix payloads, arithmetic,
deduplication and recursive levels before retained growth. No matrix dimension,
polynomial count or degree is converted into a fresh allowance. Cancellation,
ring mismatch, overflow, nonexact division and exhausted work/storage abort the
whole preparation. Storage admission covers retained payload slots, not an
unmeasured claim about Swift allocator overhead. Scratch matrices are local;
immutable outputs have identical Sendable semantics on all targets.

Verification must compare index-zero elimination against the existing independent
bivariate resultant, verify repeated-root principal coefficients, retain witnesses
for degree drops and complete fiber nullification, and reject nonexact division,
invalid axes/rings and independent ledger exhaustion. Projection preparation alone
does not prove delineability, real cell adjacency or public SSI/CS completion.

### Rational Real Cell Lifting

`OriginalRationalIntersectionCells` owns a sign-invariant cylindrical partition
of the full real parameter space for original rational equations and closed or
unrestricted coordinate domains. It consumes only a tower issued by the full
projection constructor. Domain endpoint polynomials are inserted before tower
preparation, making exact domain selection a union of whole cells. It owns
parameter-cell dimensions, not geometric image dimensions, curve joins or
singular incidence. General nonrational producers remain a separate obligation.

At axis k, every lower-level cell is connected and sign-invariant for the
projection polynomials. Specialize level-k source polynomials at its retained
exact sample and isolate all distinct real roots of each nonconstant source.
A linear source with an exact rational parent-field quotient issues that root
directly with a point isolation. Other sources use the complete Sturm issuer.
Merge these complete source root sets by exact ordering and equality. Disjoint
isolations establish order. Two original degree-one laws in the identical parent
field consume the lower ordered-field linear-sign authority at the retained
deeper root and the original leading sign for exact order or equality. Both
original admission limits are checked independently. A zero result additionally validates
the unique original linear root in their isolation intersection. This avoids
polynomial GCD/long division for that exact linear case. Other overlapping roots
establish equality only when the exact source GCD has a root in their intersection.
After computing the exact GCD of two original laws in the same field, if its
degree equals one retained original law's whole degree, divisibility proves those
root sets equal. Count the isolation intersection with that law's retained
original Sturm authority. Equality still requires count one; degree alone
never identifies roots. A proper-factor GCD keeps its own original common-law
Sturm issuer. The unique-root contracts limit intersection counts to one.
Distinct overlapping roots refine under inherited depth until separated;
subsequent `separate` always proves
disjoint rational sector brackets. A duplicate retains each original admission
domain and the maximum intersection depth; interval overlap alone never merges
roots. Known rational roots are not bisected and no aggregate product introduces
avoidable degree/coefficient growth. AdmittedIntersectionRingTests owns positive
and negative leading signs, overlapping linear order, equality depths and
independent allowance evidence.
Admission or arithmetic failure propagates without changing the allowance.
Full projection fixes each source's degree, nullification, root multiplicities,
root ordering and shared-root membership over the whole lower cell. Its ordered
continuous root sections, together with open sectors between consecutive
sections and the two unbounded sectors, cover the complete next coordinate.
Identically zero specializations are permitted only as whole-fiber nullification
under this projection certificate; no representative-only exclusion is used.

Each stack retains its parent cell, source degrees, complete root isolation and
ordered child-cell indices. Each child retains its stack, root ordinal (section)
or bounding root ordinals (sector), exact sample in one ordered-field context,
parameter dimension, inherited admission depth independent of sample-field depth,
and signs of the retained level sources. Section samples
retain original root witnesses; rational sector samples are chosen strictly
between the two roots after exact rational isolation separation. Refinement
uses the original caller's inherited depth and ledger, never a fixed search box.
Unbounded sectors use rational witnesses strictly outside the complete root
isolations; a rootless fiber uses zero as its witness. A linear section uses its
exact parent-field quotient without discarding its retained root identity or
resetting the partition's causal isolation depth. Sector depth includes both
bounding root isolations; every child inherits at least its parent's depth.

```text
original equations + exact closed-domain endpoint polynomials
 -> issued full projection tower
 -> real line roots / sections / sectors
 -> repeat over every connected lower cell
 -> full real product-domain partition
 -> exact original signs and whole-cell domain selection
```

Every child is admitted through the same parameter-cell capability even when
its sample is rational. Local invocation scratch, field arithmetic, root work,
stack/cell payloads and copied prefix arrays share one cumulative ledger.
Cancellation forwarding restores the same consumed caller state on throw.
Publication is atomic; a failed stack cannot publish an empty or partial result.
An empty solution-cell list means all complete full-dimensional partition cells
have been excluded by original equation/domain signs, not that tracing found no
representative. No cell adjacency or source-family completeness is implied.

Tests must distinguish a positive-complex-dimensional sum of squares with one
real point, a complex locus with no real points, real curves, whole-space
