# Curve Curve Contact

## Purpose and Scope

This component owns original native rational curve-pair contact receipts. Its
parent is [CADGeometry](../DESIGN.md). Its child
[Planar Partition](PlanarPartition/DESIGN.md) owns the exact planar event/cut
receipt. Loft consumes these
receipts without substituting converted control points or guide parameters.

## Responsibilities and Boundaries

Original atlas inputs additionally retain `Curve3D` and its authored closed trim.
`OriginalCurveCurveContactClassifying` consumes a caller-owned allowance that is
retained across guide segments and section queries. Native and analytic branches
prepare the original law once; singleton and closed-span jets retain the chosen
one-sided original chart. No converted spline is substituted for an angle,
lift, or implicit original parameter. General implicit cross-cell and required
differential-order coverage remains a shared lower dependency.
Invocation-owned bounded preparation and closed-span queries now consume the
shared Geometry callback port for native, original implicit, lifted and certified
families. Selected span metadata and singleton values retain original one-sided
ownership. Available caller work bounds cover storage; callback arithmetic,
localization and retained slots debit that same cumulative ledger. Existing
native/analytic precharges remain, while procedural metadata precharges are
followed by lower actual-work admission. C1-only offsets and unsupported
localizer differential orders remain explicit lower failures and do not close
complete original-family support.

An additive `OriginalCurveContactAdmission` binds this retained allowance to
the complete caller invocation: computational work is charged as units and
retention as bytes, with exact UInt32 words converted by checked multiplication
by four. The historical local combined work/retention ceiling and cell/depth
ceiling remain independent producer constraints. Local reservations precede
the caller callback, and a failed callback cannot refund or reset either
ledger. A parent with a combined work/storage counter charges retention once,
through the byte callback rather than a second work callback.

The `.line` and `.analytic(.line)` atlas representations share the same original
origin-plus-direction-times-parameter law. Exact expansion witnesses consume
their stored source coefficients directly; aliasing the two representations
does not normalize a direction or substitute a new parameter chart.
Rigid and affine image witnesses recursively evaluate the same original
homogeneous source and apply the stored matrix columns and translation by
exact expansions. Parameter images admit a candidate native parameter only
when the retained original gain's exact coefficient, offset and foreign parameter
prove the scalar correspondence exactly. Binary, ratio and product gains use
the gain owner's caller-paid exact coefficient port; product factors retain
their original identity rather than a rounded product. Rounded inverse parameters and
transformed control-point copies are not witness authority. Original native
span indexes select their raw source knots after parameter-chart recursion;
wrapper depth, retained expansion slots and arithmetic share the caller ledger.
The source wrapper limit is the same 32 levels admitted by original preparation;
it does not consume or reset parameter-cell subdivision depth.

Rational forward/inverse spatial operation words consume their immutable exact
matrix coefficient port and apply it to the original homogeneous curve law.
Native parameters are unchanged. Representable parameter witnesses compare
all three exact rational cross-weight residuals; inverse coefficients are never
rounded into floating-point expansions. Normalized algebraic operation words retain their original ordered coefficient
field and act on the original homogeneous laws in that exact field. Nested
spatial and parameter images preserve raw native parameters and selected owner
indices. Both curve laws are embedded into one retained field before forming
the three cross-weight residuals; interval coefficients never become exact
rationals. Intrinsic original-word coefficient preparation admits algebra
depth at most thirty-two, bounded by the caller's `maximumAlgebraicDepth`,
independently of pair subdivision, under the same cumulative
work, cell and UInt32-word counters. Pair projection, root isolation and
refinement retain their existing remaining pair depth and inherited causal
depth. The named preparation context restores only the depth policy.
Representable-parameter field witnesses and common-factor division consume
that same caller coefficient capability; a witness cannot restore an intrinsic
default after the caller lowered its algebra ceiling.

Affine construction charts consume the native owner's exact retained
`originalAffineParameterMap` for the original substitution t=gain*s+offset.
The owner's native index/domain refer to raw source t, while normalized
`parameters` are localization bounds only. Exact polynomial equations retain
the map. Complete contact publication must additionally prove each retained
root's closed original native-domain membership and selected-jet binding;
an outward normalized knot bracket cannot become an exact owning rectangle.
When both exact inverse native endpoints are representable binary scalars, the
upper consumer retains their exact normalized closed domain, intersects it with
the authored input trim, and requires the same native owner, cycle and map on
every selected jet. This admits an exact rectangle without substituting an
outward localization bracket. Nonrepresentable inverse cuts still require
retained scalar-root/domain clipping and produce explicit failure until that
closed admission is connected, never an empty result from mismatched domains.

Caller metadata scans use `reserveEntries` as work-unit admission. Original
payload buffers use the distinct typed storage or canonical UInt32-word entry.
Both debit the same allowance; buffer allocation is reserved before growth and
copy visits are admitted independently. A word callback cannot be routed to a
work-only callback because the parent storage owner must observe its byte cost.

Original source descent has an independent caller capability
`remainingSourceDepth` within the historical maximum of 32. Structural walkers,
exact coefficient extraction and bounded original preparation consume that
same capability. It is neither the geometric subdivision limit nor the scalar
algebra depth. Loft forwards its existing construction admission (bounded by
the contact producer's unchanged historical ceiling); root algorithms retain
their independently lowered pair and algebra policies.

A complete original fixed-coordinate component is also a continuous ambiguous
contact when the other original native parameter spans a positive interval.
Its fixed root and all free-variable coefficients are retained exactly; both
parameters need not vary. Selected original jets bind the positive weights and
owning domains. A constant spatial curve must not yield a fabricated unique
guide station.

Exact field common-factor division admits a positive original regular tube
only after all three original homogeneous equations divide exactly. This is
exact XYZ incidence under original positive weights, stronger than distance
tolerance; selected original jets retain the source/domain binding. Finite projected field roots similarly refine retained
original parameter receipts and consume selected original jets before
publication. Exact empty ideals or field zero sets cannot exclude tolerance
contact; they retain original distance exclusion or the unresolved search.

Inputs retain their original degrees, knots, homogeneous coefficients, positive
weights and selected native span ownership. Native jet preparation belongs to
CADGeometry's original curve encloser. This component owns pair-domain search,
root existence/uniqueness and original three-dimensional residual admission.
Bounded preparation and every selected closed native query forward actual
allocation bytes to the same UInt32-word ledger using checked ceiling division
by four. Metadata precharges remain in addition to actual lower reservations;
no byte callback creates a new allowance. Generated endpoint connectors charge
their construction and both retained endpoint source expressions, then consume
their corrected one-sided owning jets without inventing cross-knot continuity.
Converted Cartesian Bezier patches are never original source authority.
Guide-chain owned spans bind the declared aggregate chart through their
retained exact lower/upper station parameters and original traversal receipt.
Their raw native knot domain remains the owning source domain; it must not be
used as the aggregate contact parameter domain. A later selected query matches
the retained traversal and exact aggregate coverage before using its jet.
Nonrepresentable aggregate cuts retain the same explicit closed clipping gap
as nonrepresentable affine chart cuts.
Authored rational guide chains and endpoint corrections consume the shared
normalized original piece issuer. A retained polynomial must cover the entire
selected exact aggregate owner interval. The corrected XYZ laws include exact
endpoint differences and retain the original homogeneous denominator; native
construction coefficients and interval endpoint caches are never substituted.

Generic interval Krawczyk admission requires both retained original C0
first-order receipts on the searched trims. A union of one-sided derivative
boxes alone does not establish the continuous contraction map across a join.
Without these receipts the caller preserves the exact original algebraic
producer route and unresolved search; it does not publish a numerical regular
root or exclude a cell from a discontinuous hull. Native single-owner
polynomial cells retain their existing independent continuity authority.

    -> original native homogeneous span preparation

## Contracts and Invariants

A point receipt owns both original native parameter enclosures, its selected
span indexes and the original spatial enclosure. A continuous receipt owns both
original source curves and a proved correspondence range. Point sampling cannot
prove a root, missing contacts, continuous coincidence or separation.

An isolated result is published only when every potential native pair cell is
excluded or covered by an admitted original root authority. A continuous result
is an ambiguity witness, not a claim to enumerate every off-correspondence
contact: a proved positive-width original correspondence is sufficient to
reject a uniquely resolved Loft guide. Source identity has this same contract. A root of two projected coordinates is retained only when the
whole original third-coordinate difference meets the original distance budget;
projection selection merely steers certification. Exact absence of all three
coordinate equations proves absence of exact incidence only. It does not exclude
an unresolved projected contact whose third coordinate may remain within the
original distance tolerance. Critical projected roots therefore retain the same
full spatial residual admission; cells otherwise require original distance lower
bounds or explicit indeterminate/resource failure. This rule also applies
to full-rank projected Krawczyk, quadratic/line and stationary/line exclusions,
projected roots outside a trim, and empty finite projected root lists. Those
results steer the original exact search; none removes a still distance-admissible
three-dimensional tolerance cell. A unique projected root with inadmissible
spatial residual likewise leaves the remaining original tolerance domain
unresolved. Whole original span correspondence keeps its existing ambiguity
authority; tiny tolerance neighborhoods of transversal roots are not newly
classified as continuous source overlap. Source-identical curves may
publish identity correspondence before pair subdivision, including stationary
parameters. No rounded reconstructed spline replaces that proof.

Quadratic native curves against native equal-weight lines additionally prove
projected double-root existence and uniqueness from exact original homogeneous
expansions and a zero discriminant. The remaining spatial coordinate is still
admitted by original native enclosures. Original isolated straight native spans
through degree three additionally prove injectivity when the exact rational
derivative numerator has a constant sign on an exact subdivision cover. The
numerator is assembled from original homogeneous coefficients with positive
Bernstein denominators cleared exactly; isolated stationary speed is permitted.
Nonparallel original straight supports determine the unique spatial event, and
exact polynomial bisection retains its original parameter enclosure. Exact original knot basis polynomials additionally assemble all three
homogeneous curve-pair equations. A common factor is divided from every original
equation exactly. A nonzero signed dependent derivative and uniform opposite
edge signs on a positive-width parameter rectangle prove a real continuous
original graph, including nonaffine parameter correspondence. This receipt is
an ambiguity witness; a common factor alone is not publication authority.
A retained exact factor may also seed a regular rectangle from an exact native
parameter slice. Sturm isolation retains every root of that original slice;
refinement and outward conversion provide candidate dependent bounds only.
Publication still requires a nonzero dependent derivative over the entire
rectangle and uniform opposite signs on its two full boundary edges. Candidate
slice roots, including exact rational roots, never authorize a branch alone.
Independent-axis shrinking starts at the retained root isolation depth and
shares the caller work, cells and storage ledger. Unsuccessful candidates leave
the existing complete pair-domain search responsible for coverage.
Clipped regular branches may instead consume the algebra owner's complete
monotone-rectangle receipt. Its exact transverse-edge partition determines the
original free-axis sectors that actually contain roots, including branches
entering adjacent edges. A correspondence retains this immutable graph receipt
and a positive-width free interval inside one certified sector. It does not
extend that sector to the whole authored rectangle or claim unresolved isolated
boundary stations were enumerated. When the complete monotone receipt has no
positive-width sectors, its isolated stations promote their retained original
endpoint root and actual transverse-boundary identity directly to native UV
parameter pairs. No system is solved again. A nonzero constant original XYZ
quotient makes one selected coordinate equation equivalent to the factor;
this condition lets the complete factor zero cover certify every projected
contact on that rectangle. Promoted boundary points still pass unchanged
original three-dimensional residual admission. Multiplicities belong to the
chosen original edge/domain constraint and are not claimed as multiplicities
of the entire positive-dimensional ideal.
Original law identity also recognizes a stored line and its analytic-line alias,
or analytic circle/arc sources with identical center, normal and radius. Arc
endpoints restrict the authored domain without changing that angular law.
Identical rigid, affine or original-ratio parameter wrappers preserve these
identities recursively. `Circle3D` and analytic circles use different basis
conventions and are not inferred to have the same angular parameter chart.
A retained square-free common original factor additionally separates isolated
real critical contacts from complex positive-dimensional ideals. A nonzero
constant original quotient makes one projected equation equivalent to that
factor. A whole positive- or negative-definite original Hessian and an exact
factor/gradient zero then prove its unique real zero over the entire rectangle,
including stationary boundary contacts. Higher-order isolated stations may instead
use exact original dyadic translation: a zero constant term, uniformly signed
nonzero even-power monomials and a pure positive power of each coordinate prove
the sole real zero at the translated origin. Original lower/mid/upper stations
steer this exact identity proof; their sampled coordinates alone authorize
nothing. This handles zero Hessians without relaxing depth, work or fidelity.
An already isolated original factor/gradient system root may additionally be
promoted through the algebra owner's critical-point port. That port proves
original factor and both derivatives vanish at the retained algebraic pair.
Exact Taylor derivative signs at this same pair then prove a globally unique
real zero when all nonzero powers are even with one common sign and both pure
axis powers occur. This extends the authority to irrational flat stations
without recomputing the system, replacing its fiber, or restarting depth.
Absence of a critical zero alone never
proves absence of a real regular branch. The same immutable factor and exact XYZ
quotients serve continuous and isolated producers; preparation is charged once.

Critical finite projected ideals additionally use original homogeneous equations
and exact elimination, ordered algebraic fibers and Sturm isolation. Every
projected root retains its original parameter enclosures, including multiple
and endpoint roots; full original three-dimensional residual bounds admit or
exclude each candidate. Refinement retains source depth across calls and uses
the same work/cell/storage ledger. Exact root counts cover only the selected

Legacy periodic covers without this metadata cannot authorize an exact lift.

The retained rational atlas pair also owns one exact XYZ common factor. A

## Runtime Flows

Preparation charges original input scans, retained homogeneous coefficients and
all subsequent jet work to one invocation allowance. Native span pairs enter a
bounded stack. Exclusion precedes original projected root certification. An
unresolved cell splits its wider original parameter axis without resetting the
ledger. Duplicate roots require overlapping original parameter receipts, never
spatial proximity alone.

## State, Ownership, and Lifecycle

Input curves, native spans and receipts are immutable values owned by one call.
No shared mutable state or target-dependent synchronization is introduced.

## Failure, Concurrency, and Constraints

The default envelope retains 32 subdivisions and 1,048,576 cells. Original
coefficient preparation, retained slots and jet work share a bounded cumulative
work allowance. Overflow, nonfinite arithmetic, cancellation, unresolved
original cells and exhaustion propagate typed failure without partial output.
