## Scope and authority

This unit closes M4.P.LP: lift-specific DI consumption of the sealed periodic
Native surface/rational B-spline pcurve source family. Authored normalized T stays
one-dimensional; auxiliary UV retains independent exact surface cycles and every
closed incident side. No T period, inverse, finite cycle window, second source
formula or intersection solver is introduced.

## Behavioral evidence

- One-axis and independent two-axis Native periods preserve every closed seam,
  knot and tensor-corner side. A two-span pcurve adds both original pcurve sides
  at exact normalized T=1/3, giving independently expected four/eight incidents.
- Linear, nonmonotone quadratic and constant pcurves retain original T/UV roles,
  complete graph guards and explicit member/incident agreement. Same-XYZ UV
  shifted by a whole surface period is rejected by the authored pcurve relation.
- Strict, singleton, disconnected, out-of-support and empty requests retain exact
  support intersection. Every coordinate field is validated before empty exclusion.
- Explicit members and incidence preserve +2^96/-2^96 independent cycles without
  narrowing or finite enumeration. Original source, family and prepared-cover
  scopes end before a returned occurrence's proof/position is used again.
- Exact measured work/storage bounds succeed and one less fails independently in
  preparation and full/empty queries. Entry, intermediate and final storage-triggered
  cancellation, chart/family/source/transport limits, malformed tuples/templates/
  cycles, foreign frames and foreign algebraic coordinates propagate typed failure.
- Actual provider/source/DI graphs are compared with public original lift point
  evaluation at binary-representable T. Exact T=1/3 knot positions use an independent
  exact expected position; its binary64 approximation is not treated as the same
  authored parameter. Retained original evaluation also works after input release.

Existing finite-lift and periodic-source proofs are reused only where source,
artifact and runtime premises remain unchanged. This DI work does not claim new
normal, derivative, embedding, boundary clipping or final XYZ publication behavior.

## Diagnostics before acceptance

Initial verification failures are retained separately and are not successful
proofs: resolving swiftc into swift-driver changed driver selection; duplicate
Checks.swift basenames stopped fixture compilation; dropping a paired clang
resource-dir argument stopped Wasm linking; comparing exact 1/3 with binary64
public evaluation failed a fixture assertion. The driver/fixture repairs preserve
all production equations, resource ceilings and original equality semantics.
Diagnostic logs/commands are retained in Evidence/diagnostics; they do not establish
source-bound acceptance for their earlier uncommitted snapshots.
