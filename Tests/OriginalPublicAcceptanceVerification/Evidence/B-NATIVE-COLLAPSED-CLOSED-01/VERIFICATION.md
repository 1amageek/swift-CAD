The material restriction leaves exactly
`{(t,u,0): 0<=t<=1, 1/4<=u<=3/4}`.

| Evidence | Actual comparison |
|---|---|
| Raw completeness | Every terminal cell in the raw partition is compared iff against the independent signs of `t,t-1,u,u-1,v,v-1`. Selected cells are exactly the full original contact set, including all endpoints. |
| Refined completeness | Every terminal cell in the retained partition appears exactly once in the result. Its included flag is compared iff against the raw signs plus `u-1/4,u-3/4`. Excluded cells are checked too. |
| Raw ordinals | Every retained original raw cell ID is compared in order. Included refined cells have a valid raw ordinal whose complete six-sign signature equals their signature. Raw signatures are checked injective, so a different valid ordinal is rejected. Excluded cells require `-1`. |
