# Prepared original polyline Native surface lift

## Scope and original authority

This unit binds an actual SurfaceLiftCurve3D to an already-certified complete
OriginalPolylinePcurveSnapshot and the existing Native surface snapshot producer.
The same pcurve owner and exact Euclidean arc-length field survive source binding,
graph preparation and DI queries. The source never rebuilds its length roots or
rounds its stations. Exact point-by-point input matching and finite-axis vertex
inclusion prove the original construction and whole affine segment support.
Periodic axes retain unrestricted authored UV; the existing fundamental source
producer proves cycles before periodic graph publication.

General hosts, priority images, tolerance-only support clamps, automatic
rolling-ball/M7 dispatch and full M4-M8/whole-seven/full87 completion remain open.
The specialized refusals keep explicit incomplete-implementation markers.

## Targets and receipts

Each lane freshly compiles the two new source files and the existing current
polyline/retained-curve/Native-chart/lift-graph/DI dependencies selected by Verify.py.
Generated assembly files only prepend original-module imports. No production
visibility, solver, equation or representation is rewritten. The original
coefficient algebra, spline law/period issuers, transport/cover and numerical
children are reused from hash-admitted generation-bound caches, checked before
and after execution through the previously accepted polyline source receipts.

Fixed Swift6.4.0 and matching SDKs compile/link/run Native, changed-assembly ASAN,
normal WASM and Embedded WASM. Every process has a120-second timeout. Runtime is
Native0.982s/ASAN1.190s/normal-WASM1.015s/Embedded0.525s; Embedded link47.280s is
the critical path. ASAN instruments the fresh assembly and fixture, not unchanged
cached numerical/algebraic children. No broad production CAD or L2-L6 build occurs.
Exploratory missing assembly type, borrowed-capture and fixture-depth diagnostics
and the corrected huge-cycle incident expectation are preserved under `.build`;
they are excluded from acceptance. Accepted logs contain no Swift warnings/errors,
sanitizer failure, null call or signature mismatch.
