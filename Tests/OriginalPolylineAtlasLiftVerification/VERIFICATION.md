existing original Coons and boundary-correction atlas providers, retained child

## Behavioral evidence

boundary. Both Coons and corrected hosts consume a repeated-point/folded polyline
whose first join is sqrt(2)/(sqrt(2)+2). The original graph returns all twelve or
three chart products, eight or two join incidents, exact authored T/UV and XYZ.
Independent expected laws distinguish the Coons linear blend from the correction
quintic 10v^3-15v^4+6v^5. The public numerical point is checked at five stations as
a regression, not as a source equality certificate. Wrong in-scope UV is excluded.

polyline-lift receipts. Every compile/link/runtime process has a120-second timeout.
