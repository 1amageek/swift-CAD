# Original Native rolling-ball source and domain

This unit implements the three-nonperiodic-Native-child source and domain slice
of M4.S.RB. Complete general rolling-ball supply, including lifted/non-Native rails,
remains open with M4.S.C. It does not complete fillet, M5 composition, general M6,
automatic M7 publication, M8 or whole-seven/full87 acceptance.

## Actual implementation path

The provider retains the real rolling-ball construction and its three separately
bound child curves. Native family preparation reuses the existing source issuers.
The new source-owned graph producer translates the original minor-arc expression,
retaining positive radial lengths q1/q2 and positive half-angle w as auxiliaries,
with the exact equations and all denominator, radius, normalization and regular-arc
guards. No root is rounded into a coefficient. The original numerical feasibility
threshold is read from the evaluator, never used as geometric equality or identity.

Complete sealed child families establish original `[0,1]` support. The preparer
retains every nonempty closed triple intersection, including singleton incidents;
no equal-degree/knot/weight or translated-rail prerequisite is imposed. DI verifies
frame support, source ownership and authored semantics, retains the request and
its exact effective enclosure, and consumes each graph through the existing
binder. Graph parameters remain UV; the three auxiliaries do not change source arity.

`Verify.py` recompiles actual production bodies with imports-only assembly into
`RollingBallAdapters`. The real constructor/point evaluation, original regularity
validator/enclosure and strict decoding body accompany the new source/DI files.
The unchanged CAD children come from hash-admitted immutable M6 products. Newly
assembled production and fixtures are ASAN-instrumented; cached unchanged children
are not. This is neither a broad CAD/L2-L6 build nor ordinary Surface3D/Kernel
fillet dispatch verification. Those obligations remain explicitly open.
