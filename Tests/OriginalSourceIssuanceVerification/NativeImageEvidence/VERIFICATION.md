## Behavior and failure evidence

Native laws compare complete coefficient polynomials, including weighted, tensor,
nonunit and subnormal inputs. Periodic laws prove original periods, repeated
controls, exact translations and unchanged authored parameter transport. Tests
reject approximate period replacement, changed repeated controls and foreign
bindings. Actual periodic provider branches execute on curve and tensor inputs.

Image laws use exact inversion of the original binary frame, including a sheared
nonunit fixture; radius products are exact. Physical angles retain original
radian bits and exact rational functions of tan(a/2). Polynomial identities prove
sin^2+cos^2=1 and quotient equality. Rational bounds distinguish original sin(1)
from consecutive binary64 neighbors. Negative angles and repeated angle doubling
exercise range reduction. Cone tests compare the whole image polynomial and
exactly substitute original points on both nappes and the apex; an off-image
point is excluded by a proved sign. Original inputs can be released or mutated
without changing retained laws.

Independently measured exact work/storage boundaries succeed; one-unit lower
limits fail. Entry/final cancellation, coefficient-depth refusal, foreign chart/
field owners, zero divisors, invalid angles and singular frames fail explicitly.
No test uses residual agreement as an existence proof or returns partial success.
