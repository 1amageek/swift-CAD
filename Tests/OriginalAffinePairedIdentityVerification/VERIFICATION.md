## Scope and authority

M5.BA lifts an existing sealed child identity through independently bound affine
parent covers after exact source-owned position-action agreement. All three existing
wrapper families supply actions: parameter image, spatial word and directed traversal.
No position formula or shape producer is duplicated. The output is the existing
identity receipt and current relation-view/query consumers read it. The single-cover
API delegates to the same implementation and retains its original success behavior.

Design authorities: [source issuance](../../Sources/CADGeometry/OriginalSourceIssuance/DESIGN.md#original-affine-position-action-supply)
and [identity](../../Sources/CADGeometry/OriginalDomainIdentity/OriginalParameterIdentity/DESIGN.md#paired-affine-child-identity-transport).
General nonlinear/singular transport, new join proofs and unequal-action scoped
position theorems remain M5.C. Required unsupported inputs remain incomplete there;
transport failure is not a nonintersection or refuted source-equality result.

The independent graph compiles actual current L0/L1/DI0/source/DI files and original
reading-protocol fixtures. Native stored arrays and analytic construction inputs
pass the actual source-law/domain/identity issuers. Curve3D/Surface3D adapters, new
Kernel callers, general M6 and automatic M7 routes are not executed in this unit.
Their earlier evidence is not generalized to the new paired API. Full M4-M8 and
whole-seven/full87 completion remain open.

## Behavioral evidence

- Two original tensor surfaces have whole-law matching declared boundaries and
  unequal transverse derivatives. Distinct source owners, independently reversed/
  exchanged/nonuniform parent axes and nonidentity spatial actions retain exact
  correspondence. Nonidentity child permutations are also exercised. Parent gains
  independently expected as -3/4 and -1/2 agree; wrong in-scope tuples are refuted.
- Nested paired covers, symmetry and transitive composition retain source depths,
  proof kind and both scopes. A foreign intermediate owner is rejected. No tangent,
  normal or higher-order continuity is inferred from position identity.
- Independent right-side strict/disconnected/singleton/empty requests constrain
  both scopes through complete pullback. Same-source foreign frames and foreign
  child/action bindings fail. Empty publication obeys terminal cancellation.
- A translation difference of exactly 2^-50 rejects action agreement without tolerance
  merging. Absent action and an explicit identity matrix agree. Unequal matrices
  need an additional source theorem even if they might agree on some boundary.
- Source action preparation verifies exact work/storage and one-less refusals,
  entry/mid/final cancellation, checked coefficient indices and all three readers.
  Caller array mutation leaves retained source coefficients unchanged.
- Paired preparation succeeds at Native work1549/storage9728 and fails at one less.
  Source/chart/transport/proof limits remain independent. Entry/mid/final cancellation
  preserves direct, source or domain error wrappers; no success or retry is produced.
- An instrumented exact rational field marks a position-action translation with
  independent value/coefficient depth7. The translation cancels from the final
  parameter correspondence, but later query, symmetry and composition still require7
  and reject6. This proves causal admission retention, not a new coefficient capability.
- Physical-radian periods retain mathematical pi; rounded 2*Double.pi is refuted.
  A foreign action field fails explicitly. Collapsed sphere products retain all UV
  and near-pole tuples remain outside the identity scope.
- Local construction scopes end before later receipt use. Original proofs/covers/
  actions remain owned, public constraint COW edits cannot mutate evidence, and
  rational relation views use zero generated storage. Existing single-cover tests
  all pass after delegation; its Native storage increases from8160 to8176 to account
