## Behavior and counterexamples

- Actual affine, constant-U/V, reversed and multiturn pcurves preserve exact original
  T/UV and all seam/corner sides on one/two independently periodic axes. Every
  applicable graph satisfies its original guards and real public evaluation agrees
  at the selected exactly representable inputs. Wrong UV shifted by a whole host
  period is rejected even when its XYZ coincides.
- Cancellation-sensitive origin/direction/traversal coefficients retain their exact
  expression rather than rounded endpoint samples. This case checks exact source
  graph UV; it does not equate intermediate binary64 numerical rounding with source
  equality or claim a new public numerical accuracy guarantee.
- Arbitrary-size cycles, strict/singleton/disconnected/empty requests, foreign fields/
  owners, independent resource/depth/chart/family limits, entry/intermediate/final
  cancel and source/cover-release lifetime run through the same DI assertions.
- Retained family preparation succeeds at exact measured work/storage and fails at
  one less. Foreign child authority fails, and the nonperiodic-axis tolerance clamp
  remains explicitly refused by the actual snapshot provider.
- A genuine retained algebraic pcurve exposes a coefficient-field capability gap:
  rational hosting fields must refuse its sqrt(2) coefficient before lazy family
  publication. A matching algebraic hosting field issues and evaluates the same
  original graph successfully. All consumed support/polynomial coefficients are
  admitted into the host field during shared preparation.
- Current independent and actual original Native periodic source/DI fixture bodies
  execute again, including nonlinear/constant pcurves, all incident counts, typed
  Native pcurve view, finite weighted lift regression, old periodic finite refusal,
  COW/source lifetime and resource/cancel behavior.
